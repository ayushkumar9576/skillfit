# Roadmap

This roadmap outlines the high-level development direction of the SkillFit Placement Intelligence Platform.

> Note: Features and priorities may evolve as the project matures.

---

## ✅ Completed

### Phase 1 — Foundation & Architecture
Core platform setup, system architecture, database design, and development infrastructure.

### Phase 2 — Authentication & User Management
Secure authentication, user management, and role-based access control.

### Phase 3 — Resume Intelligence
Intelligent resume processing pipeline capable of transforming resumes into structured candidate intelligence profiles.

---

## 🚧 In Progress

### Phase 4 — Placement Drive Intelligence
Building the intelligence layer for understanding placement opportunities, eligibility requirements, and recruiter expectations.

---

## 📌 Planned

### Phase 5 — Matching Intelligence
Intelligent comparison between candidate profiles and placement opportunities.

### Phase 6 — Student Experience
Personalized insights, recommendations, and readiness tracking for students.

### Phase 7 — Faculty Experience
Monitoring, analytics, and mentoring tools for faculty members.

### Phase 8 — Platform Integration
End-to-end integration of all core workflows into a unified system.

### Phase 9 — External Intelligence
Integration of external professional and coding profiles to enrich candidate evaluation.

### Phase 10 — Advanced Skill Intelligence
Multi-source skill validation and evidence-driven capability assessment.

### Phase 11 — Predictive Intelligence
Machine learning models for recommendations, forecasting, and risk identification.

### Phase 12 — Knowledge Intelligence
Retrieval and knowledge systems for placement-related insights and decision support.

### Phase 13 — AI Placement Advisor
Conversational AI assistant for students, faculty, and placement teams.

---

## Long-Term Vision

SkillFit aims to become a comprehensive placement intelligence platform that helps:

- Students understand and improve their employability.
- Faculty monitor and guide student progress.
- Placement teams identify opportunities and track readiness.
- Organizations discover candidates aligned with their requirements.

The platform combines structured data, artificial intelligence, predictive analytics, and intelligent recommendations to support data-driven placement decisions.
